Your first enterprise deal is usually the first time you have to take security seriously — 85 to 95 percent of enterprise buyers require SOC 2. So you hire one security person. Realistically, they can run one pentest a year: it takes six to ten weeks from booking to report and costs around eighteen thousand dollars. Meanwhile your engineers ship every day, increasingly with AI — and Veracode found 45% of AI-generated code has security flaws.
